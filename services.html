Sleep support that starts with understanding.

SleepTots is built around the idea that meaningful sleep support should feel personal. Instead of beginning with a rigid formula, we begin by listening to your family, understanding your child's current patterns, and identifying where your evenings feel most difficult.

Thoughtful boundaries

Sleep support should complement, not replace, professional care.

SleepTots services are designed to provide practical routine and parent-support guidance. They are not a substitute for medical assessment or treatment. When a concern involves your child's health, illness, breathing, feeding, growth, or another medical issue, speak with your paediatrician or appropriate healthcare professional.

Frequently asked questions

A few things parents often want to know first.

Start here for a quick overview of how SleepTots services are structured.

How do I know which service is right for us?

Start with the stage or challenge that most closely reflects what your family is experiencing. During the initial conversation, your situation can be explored in more detail so the support pathway is clear.

Is newborn support about putting my baby on a strict schedule?

No. Newborn support focuses on understanding your baby's current rhythm, creating calming routines, and helping parents navigate the early months with realistic expectations.

What if my child's sleep situation changes after we begin?

Change is part of family life. Support is designed to help you review what is happening, identify what needs adjusting, and keep the overall approach manageable rather than starting over from scratch.

Can support be adapted to our family routine?

Yes. The intention is to make recommendations fit your family's practical circumstances, preferences, and priorities instead of asking your household to fit into a generic routine.

What happens in the first conversation?

You can talk through your child's current sleep patterns, your biggest challenges, what you have already tried, and what you would like bedtime to feel like. From there, the next step can be explained clearly.
