Common Challenges

Sleep Struggles You Don't Have to Face Alone

Many parents experience similar sleep challenges. Recognizing them is the first step toward restful nights for the whole family.

Frequent Night Wakings

Your baby wakes multiple times each night, leaving everyone exhausted. We help identify the causes and build a gentle plan to consolidate sleep.

Short Naps & Catnapping

Daytime naps lasting only 30–45 minutes can disrupt nighttime sleep too. We guide you in extending naps and establishing age-appropriate schedules.

Sleep Regressions

Just when things seemed on track, sleep falls apart. Regressions are developmental leaps — we help you navigate them with confidence.

Our Philosophy

Gentle, Responsive, and Rooted in Trust

We believe that healthy sleep habits are built on connection, not separation. Our approach never involves leaving your child to cry alone. Instead, we use responsive, gradual methods that respect your child's emotional needs.

  • No cry-it-out or controlled crying methods
  • Age-appropriate sleep expectations
  • Respects breastfeeding, co-sleeping, and family preferences
  • Builds long-term, independent sleep skills gently
  • Supports both child and parental well-being
